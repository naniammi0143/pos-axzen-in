FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything You Need to Know

What is axzen POS? +
axzen POS is an all-in-one point-of-sale solution that helps businesses manage billing, products, inventory, customers, sales and reports from one platform.
Can I use axzen POS for my restaurant? +
Yes. axzen POS is designed for restaurants, cafes, retail stores, grocery shops, food trucks, bakeries and many other businesses.
Does it support thermal printers? +
axzen POS is designed to work with compatible Bluetooth thermal printers, including commonly used 58mm receipt printers.
How can I get started? +
Click Request Demo and submit your business details. Our team will contact you to understand your requirements and help you get started.
